## Full description (4000)
Nerva is the focus workspace that never forgets — and it stays on your phone.

FOCUS TIMERS THAT SURVIVE ANYTHING
Start a 25-minute sprint or a 2-hour deep-work block. Long sessions get automatic Pomodoro breaks (25/5, longer every fourth). Timers use wall-clock maths, so they are still right after sleep, reboot or days in the background. Home-screen widget with play/pause.

HABITS, THE ATOMIC HABITS WAY
One tap logs a habit. Give each habit a cue (“after coffee”), a two-minute version for bad days and the identity it supports (“I am a reader”). See misses in a row so you never miss twice, milestones at 7 / 21 / 66 / 100 / 365 days, a year heatmap and your weekday pattern. Optional daily reminder per habit.

REMINDERS THAT REACH YOU
“Remind me” once, daily, on weekdays or weekly. Task deadlines alert too. Exact, screen-off alarms with snooze.

TASKS AND NOTES
Swipe to complete, due dates, priorities. Markdown notes with a home-screen note widget.

WORKS WITH GEMINI (ANDROID 16+)
Nerva registers Android App Functions, so once you enable it under Gemini → Connected apps you can just say:
• “Focus for 25 minutes on the report”
• “Pause my timer” · “Resume”
• “Add a task: renew passport” · “I finished the landlord email”
• “I did my reading today” · “Log two glasses of water”
• “Remind me to call Sam at 5 pm”
• “What’s next today?”
No API key, no account. Everything runs on your phone.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No cloud. Your timers, notes, tasks and habits live in a local database on your device. The app sends one anonymous daily launch count (version, OS, language — no identifier) which you can switch off. Nothing you write ever leaves the phone unless you connect your own Google Drive (optional Pro sync, limited to the single file Nerva creates).

OPEN SOURCE
Apache-2.0. Full source on GitHub. Same core as Nerva for Linux and Windows.

Free forever for everything in the app today. An optional Pro licence (bought on nerva.bytical.ai) funds development and adds Google Drive sync across devices.
